Location Photos
---------------
Place photos of the office building and location here.

Recommended files:
- building-exterior.jpg  (main exterior shot, min 1200px wide)
- building-interior.jpg  (reception or office interior, optional)
- building-sign.jpg      (suite/door sign, optional)

Usage:
These images are referenced in the Contact page.
Update app/[locale]/contact/page.tsx to swap the placeholder
once real photos are provided.
